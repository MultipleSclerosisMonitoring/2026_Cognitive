Clinical comparison
===================

The clinical comparison is descriptive and inferential. It compares affected
participants (``EM``) with healthy controls (``Controles``) for digital and
paper SDMT/TMT outcomes. For each outcome it reports means, medians, Welch's
t-test, and Mann-Whitney U. The adjusted model is OLS with HC3 covariance and
includes age, sex, and education where available.

Because the control group is small and outcomes are missing unevenly, every
summary reports the effective ``N``. Adjusted p-values are corrected across
available adjusted outcomes with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR. The report is not a
replacement for a prespecified clinical validation study.
